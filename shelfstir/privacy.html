Privacy Policy

Shelf & Stir for Android · Effective 9 November 2026 (updated for AI Scan and Create)

Privacy Policy Terms of Use Licences & Credits

This policy explains what the Shelf & Stir app (“Shelf & Stir”, “we”, “us”) does with information. Shelf & Stir is made by Coastline Machine Management in New South Wales, Australia. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

In short: there's no account and we don't collect your name or email. With AI Scan and Create on, your shelf photo or ingredient list is sent to our AI service only to find your bottles or create drinks, and isn't stored by us. Turn on Private mode and photos are read on your phone only. Free users watch ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to show them.

Your photos and AI Scan

  • When you take or choose a photo, Shelf & Stir first reads the label text on your phone using Google ML Kit, built into the app.
  • With AI Scan (on unless you turn on Private mode), a reduced copy of the photo is also sent over an encrypted connection to our server function (hosted by Supabase in Japan) and on to Anthropic's Claude AI (in the United States), which lists the bottles it can see. The result comes back to your phone. We don't store the photo, and it isn't linked to you.
  • Anthropic processes it as our service provider under its commercial terms, which say it doesn't use this data to train its models. It may keep requests for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring, as described in its terms.
  • Photos taken with the camera are held in the app's private temporary storage only while they're read, and deleted the next time you take a photo or when Android clears the app's cache. Photos you choose from your gallery stay where they are.
  • ML Kit sends Google basic diagnostic information (for example phone model, Android version, app version and how long reading took). It never includes your photo or the words read from it. See ML Kit's data disclosure.
  • Private mode (Settings) turns AI Scan and Create off: photos are then read on your phone only and nothing about them leaves it.

Create

When you use Create (part of the Pro + Create plan), the app sends our AI service your bottle names, pantry items, the fruit and extras you pick or type, your choices (mood, strength, occasion) and the names and star ratings of drinks you've rated, so it can suggest drinks you'll like. Please don't type personal information into the “anything else” box. This is processed the same way as AI Scan and isn't stored by us. Recipes you keep are stored on your phone only.

Usage limits

To keep AI costs fair, the app sends a random install ID (created on your phone, not linked to your name, Google account or advertising ID) with each AI request. Our server keeps only a daily count of requests per install ID, deleted after 90 days.

What the app keeps on your phone

Your list of bottles (“My bar”), your pantry ticks, saved favourites, My creations and ratings, unit choice, Private mode setting and your answer to the age question are stored only on your phone, inside the app. We can't see them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Advertising: Google AdMob

On the free plan you watch a short video ad from Google AdMob to reveal your cocktails after each scan. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (from the IP address), device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens and up, and they are not targeted using anything from your photos or your bar.

Where the law requires it (for example the EU, UK and some US states) you're asked for consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads). How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Pro and Pro + Create members don't see ads, and the app doesn't request ads for them.

Purchases: Google Play

Pro (A$1.99 a month, A$14.99 a year or A$29.99 once) and Pro + Create (A$4.99 a month), or the local prices shown in Google Play, are sold and billed by Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never receive your name, card or bank details. The app only learns which plan your Google account has. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase: policies.google.com/privacy.

Permissions

  • Internet: for AI Scan and Create, ads and Google Play billing.
  • Shelf & Stir does not ask for camera, storage, location, contacts or microphone permission. Photos are taken with your phone's own camera app and chosen with Android's photo picker, so the app only sees the one photo you hand it.

Sharing a recipe

If you tap Share on a recipe, the recipe text goes to the app you pick (for example Messages or WhatsApp), under that app's own privacy policy.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent data, run our own analytics or crash reporting, use your data to build a profile of you, or share anything with other companies apart from the service providers described above: Anthropic and Supabase for AI Scan and Create, and Google for ads, billing and ML Kit diagnostics.

Age

Shelf & Stir is about alcoholic drinks and is only for adults of legal drinking age (18 or over in Australia, or older where your local law requires it). The app asks your age when you first open it and closes if you're under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a child has used the app, contact us and we'll help.

Deleting your data

Everything Shelf & Stir keeps about your bar and drinks is on your phone. You can clear your bar in Settings › Clear my bar, or remove everything by uninstalling the app. There is no account to delete. The only thing on our server is the anonymous daily request count, deleted after 90 days. To cancel a subscription, go to Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

Security

Your bar and drinks stay on your phone, protected by its own security. All connections (our AI service, Google ads and billing) are encrypted, and the AI key is held on our server, never in the app.

Overseas processing

AI requests are processed by Supabase (Japan) and Anthropic (United States). We use these providers only to run the features you use, under their contractual privacy and security commitments.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Questions or privacy complaints: info@mechiq.com.au. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
