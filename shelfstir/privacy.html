Privacy Policy

Shelf & Stir for Android · Effective 9 November 2026

Privacy Policy Terms of Use Licences & Credits

This policy explains what the Shelf & Stir app (“Shelf & Stir”, “we”, “us”) does with information. Shelf & Stir is made by Coastline Machine Management in New South Wales, Australia. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

In short: your shelf photos are read on your phone and never uploaded. There's no account and we don't collect your name, email or photos. Free users watch an ad from Google AdMob to see their results, and Google collects some device information to show it, as explained below.

Your photos

  • When you take or choose a photo, Shelf & Stir reads the words on the bottle labels on your phone, using Google ML Kit text recognition that is built into the app. The photo is not sent to us, to Google or to anyone else.
  • Photos you take with the camera are held in the app's private temporary storage only while they're read, and are deleted the next time you take a photo or when Android clears the app's cache. Photos you choose from your gallery stay where they are; the app only reads them.
  • We never receive, store or see your photos.

What the app keeps on your phone

Your list of bottles (“My bar”), your pantry ticks, saved favourites, unit choice and your answer to the age question are stored only on your phone, inside the app. We can't see them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Advertising: Google AdMob

On the free plan you watch a short video ad from Google AdMob to reveal your cocktails after each scan. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (from the IP address), device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens and up, and they are not targeted using anything from your photos or your bar.

Where the law requires it (for example the EU, UK and some US states) you're asked for consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads). How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Shelf & Stir Plus subscribers don't see ads, and the app doesn't request ads for them.

Subscriptions: Google Play

Shelf & Stir Plus (A$0.99 a month, or the local equivalent shown in Google Play) is sold and billed by Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never receive your name, card or bank details. The app only learns whether your Google account has an active Plus subscription. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase: policies.google.com/privacy.

Permissions

  • Internet: only for ads and Google Play billing.
  • Shelf & Stir does not ask for camera, storage, location, contacts or microphone permission. Photos are taken with your phone's own camera app and chosen with Android's photo picker, so the app only sees the one photo you hand it.

Sharing a recipe

If you tap Share on a recipe, the recipe text goes to the app you pick (for example Messages or WhatsApp), under that app's own privacy policy.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent data, run our own analytics or crash reporting, use your data to build a profile of you, or share anything with other companies apart from what Google collects for ads and billing as described above.

Age

Shelf & Stir is about alcoholic drinks and is only for adults of legal drinking age (18 or over in Australia, or older where your local law requires it). The app asks your age when you first open it and closes if you're under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a child has used the app, contact us and we'll help.

Deleting your data

Everything Shelf & Stir keeps is on your phone. You can clear your bar in Settings › Clear my bar, or remove everything by uninstalling the app. There is no account to delete and nothing held by us. To cancel Plus, go to Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

Security

Because your photos and bar never leave your phone, they're protected by your phone's own security. Connections to Google for ads and billing are encrypted.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Questions or privacy complaints: info@mechiq.com.au. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
