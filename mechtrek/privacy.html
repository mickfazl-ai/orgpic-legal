This policy explains what the MechTrek app (“MechTrek”, “we”, “us”) does with your information. MechTrek is a car logbook for Australian tax records, made by Coastline Machine Management, Australia.

In short

MechTrek records your car trips so it can build your logbook. Your trips are stored on your phone and backed up to your private MechTrek account so you don't lose them. Only you can see them. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use it for anything except running your logbook.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, and your name and profile picture if you sign in with Google. Used only to sign you in and to reset your password.
  • Location (including in the background): while a trip is being recorded, MechTrek uses your phone's GPS to measure the distance and record the route, start and end points. With automatic logging on, this happens even when the app is closed — by default only during the work hours you set. Location is not collected when no trip is being recorded.
  • Physical activity: Android's activity recognition tells MechTrek when you start and stop driving, so trips can be logged automatically. Only the "in a vehicle" signal is used.
  • Logbook details you enter: vehicles (make, model, rego, engine, odometer), work hours and workplaces, saved places, trip purposes and any edits.
  • Street addresses: start and end points are turned into street addresses (with suburb and postcode) using your phone's built-in Android geocoder, which may contact Google to do the lookup.

How it's used

Only to provide MechTrek: recording and sorting your trips into work and personal, showing your routes and reports, exporting your logbook, and keeping it backed up and in step across your devices.

Where it's stored

On your phone, and in your MechTrek account hosted by Supabase (data centre in Tokyo, Japan). Every record is locked to your account so only you can read it. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Maps are shown with Google Maps.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share your data, and we don't use it for advertising or analytics. Data only goes elsewhere when you choose to export or share your logbook (for example emailing a spreadsheet to your accountant).

Your choices

  • Turn automatic logging off at any time in the app, or remove MechTrek's location and physical-activity permissions in Android settings.
  • Edit or delete any trip in the app.
  • Export your logbook to Excel or CSV at any time.
  • Delete your account: in the app go to Setup › Delete account and data, or see how to delete your account. This permanently removes your account and all trips, vehicles and settings from our servers.

Keeping data

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our database straight away (backups held by our hosting provider roll off within 30 days).

Children

MechTrek is for drivers and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact
