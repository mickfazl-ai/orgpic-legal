This policy explains what the PassPic app (“PassPic”, “we”, “us”) does with information. PassPic is made by Coastline Machine Management, Australia.

In short

Your photos never leave your phone unless you choose to share or save them somewhere. We don't have accounts and we don't collect your photos or personal details. The free option shows an ad from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to show it — explained below.

Your photos

  • Photos you take or choose are processed entirely on your device, including the alignment guides and background removal (Google ML Kit runs on the phone; nothing is uploaded).
  • Finished photos are saved only where you choose in Settings: your gallery (Pictures › PassPic), a folder you pick, or a place you choose each time (for example Google Drive or OneDrive).
  • We never receive, store or see your photos.

Advertising — Google AdMob

To get a free photo you can watch a short video ad from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. In the EU, UK and other places that require it, you're asked for consent first; you can change your choice any time in PassPic › Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings › Google › Ads. How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases — Google Play

The HD Pack is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never receive your card or bank details. The app only learns whether the HD Pack has been bought on your Google account.

Permissions

  • Camera — to show the live preview with alignment guides and take the photo.
  • Internet — only for ads and Google Play purchases.

PassPic does not ask for your location, contacts, microphone or files. Saving uses Android's own folder and file pickers, so PassPic only touches the places you choose.

Printing and sharing

When you print or share, the photo goes to the printer service or app you pick, under that service's own privacy policy.

What we don't do

We don't sell data, use analytics or crash-reporting services of our own, or build a profile of you.

Children

PassPic is not directed at children. A parent or guardian should take and save photos for a child's passport.

Deleting your data

Everything PassPic keeps is on your phone. Uninstalling PassPic removes its settings; photos you saved stay where you put them until you delete them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: info@mechiq.com.au.
