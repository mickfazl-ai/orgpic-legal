Privacy Policy

Giftrix for Android · Effective 9 November 2026

Privacy Policy Terms of Use Licences & Credits

This policy explains what the Giftrix app (“Giftrix”, “we”, “us”) does with information. Giftrix is made by Coastline Machine Management in New South Wales, Australia. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

In short: there's no account and we don't collect your name or email. What you type about the person you're buying for is sent to our AI service to suggest presents and search stores, and isn't stored by us or linked to you. Free users watch ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to show them.

What you type and the AI

  • To suggest interests, present ideas and where to buy them, the app sends our server function (hosted by Supabase) what you entered: who the present is for (for example “Son”), their gender and age or stage of life, an optional first name, the occasion, their interests, any notes, your budget and the kinds of present you picked. Our server passes this to Anthropic's Claude AI (in the United States), which may run web searches for products in Australian stores.
  • Please only enter a first name or nickname, and don't type anything sensitive (like health details, addresses or phone numbers) into the notes.
  • We don't store your requests. Anthropic processes them as our service provider under its commercial terms, which say it doesn't use this data to train its models; it may keep requests for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring.
  • To make repeat searches faster and cheaper, our server keeps the store results for a present idea (the search phrase, budget band, rough age band and gender, and the stores and prices found) for 24 hours. These contain no name, notes or anything that identifies you.

Usage limits

To keep AI costs fair, the app sends a random install ID (created on your phone, not linked to your name, Google account or advertising ID) with each request. Our server keeps only a daily count of requests per install ID, deleted after 90 days.

What the app keeps on your phone

Your saved presents and recent searches are stored only on your phone, inside the app. We can't see them. You can clear them in Settings, and uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Advertising: Google AdMob

On the free plan you watch a short video ad from Google AdMob to unlock present ideas and store searches. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (from the IP address), device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens and up and are not targeted using anything you type into Giftrix.

Where the law requires it (for example the EU, UK and some US states) you're asked for consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads). How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Pro members don't see ads, and the app doesn't request ads for them.

Store links and commissions

When you tap a store, it opens in your browser or the store's app, and that store's own privacy policy applies. Some links (for example Amazon) may include our affiliate tag, so we may earn a small commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you. The affiliate tag doesn't tell us who you are or what you bought.

Purchases: Google Play

Giftrix Pro (A$1.99 a month or A$14.99 a year, or the local prices shown in Google Play) is sold and billed by Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never receive your name, card or bank details. The app only learns whether your Google account has Pro. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase: policies.google.com/privacy.

Permissions

Giftrix only uses Internet access (for present ideas, store searches, ads and Google Play billing). It doesn't ask for camera, storage, location, contacts or microphone permission.

Sharing an idea

If you tap Share, the idea and store links go to the app you pick (for example Messages or WhatsApp), under that app's own privacy policy.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent data, run our own analytics or crash reporting, build a profile of you, or share anything with other companies apart from the service providers described above: Anthropic and Supabase for the AI features, and Google for ads and billing.

Age

Giftrix is made for adults buying presents, including presents for children. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a child has used the app, contact us and we'll help.

Deleting your data

Your saved presents and searches are on your phone: clear them in Settings › Clear saved presents and history, or uninstall the app. There's no account to delete. The only things on our server are the anonymous daily request count (deleted after 90 days) and anonymous store results (deleted after 24 hours). To cancel Pro, go to Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

Security and overseas processing

All connections are encrypted, and the AI key is held on our server, never in the app. AI requests are processed by Supabase and Anthropic, which may be outside Australia (including the United States and Japan), under their contractual privacy and security commitments.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Questions or privacy complaints: info@mechiq.com.au. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
