Privacy & Data Policy Terms of Use Delete your account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open MechTrek and go to Setup.
  2. In the account card at the top, tap Delete account and data.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted straight away.

Tip: export your logbook first (Report › Export to Excel) if you need it for your tax records.

By email

If you can't open the app, email info@mechiq.com.au from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my MechTrek account". We'll delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Delete some data but keep your account

  • A trip: open it in Trips, tap Edit, then Delete trip. It's removed from your phone and your MechTrek account.
  • A vehicle and its logbook: Setup › Vehicles › choose the vehicle › Delete vehicle (this also deletes its trips).
  • Saved places and work hours: edit or remove them in Setup.
  • Or email info@mechiq.com.au and tell us what to delete; we'll do it within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your MechTrek sign-in (email address, and Google profile details if you used Google).
  • All trips, routes, addresses, vehicles, work hours, places and settings.

Nothing is kept. Copies in our hosting provider's backups roll off within 30 days.
