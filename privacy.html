This policy explains what the OrgPic app (“OrgPic”, “we”, “us”) does with information. OrgPic is a camera app that files photos and videos into projects and folders on your own device.

In short

OrgPic does not collect, send or sell any information about you. Your photos, videos, project names and settings stay on your device. OrgPic has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access.

What OrgPic stores, and where

  • Photos and videos you take or add are saved on your device in Pictures › OrgPic › your project › your folder, where your gallery and file apps can also see them.
  • Your project and folder names, and camera settings (flash, white balance, brightness, anti-flicker, HDR, grid), are kept in OrgPic's private storage on your device.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else.

Permissions

  • Camera — to take photos and videos.
  • Microphone — only to record sound with videos. If you say no, videos record without sound.
  • Photos and videos — OrgPic works with the files it creates. To add other photos (for example ones synced from smart glasses through the Meta AI app), you choose them in Android's own photo picker, and only those are copied in. When you move or delete a file OrgPic didn't create, Android asks you first.

OrgPic does not ask for your location, contacts, or any other permission, and it has no internet permission.

What OrgPic does not do

OrgPic does not collect personal information, track you, show ads, use analytics or crash-reporting services, or share data with third parties. It does not add your location to photos. (Photos you import from elsewhere keep whatever details they already contain.)

Sharing

Nothing leaves OrgPic unless you tap Share. Shared photos go to the app you pick (email, messages, cloud storage and so on), and that app's own privacy policy then applies.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include OrgPic's project list and settings in your device backup so they come back on a new phone. That backup is managed by Google under your account; we can't see it. Your photos are only backed up if your own gallery or cloud app (such as Google Photos or OneDrive) does it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your files stay on your device until you delete them — in OrgPic or any gallery app. Uninstalling OrgPic removes its project list and settings, but leaves your photos and videos in Pictures › OrgPic. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on request.

Children

OrgPic is a work tool for adults and isn't directed at children under 13.

Security

Because your data never leaves your device, it is protected by your device's own security (screen lock and encryption).

Your privacy rights

We don't collect personal information as defined in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), so we hold nothing about you to access, correct or delete. If you have a question or a concern, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with an updated date and shown in the app.
